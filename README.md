# UK triple lock: cost, distribution and uncertainty

PolicyEngine UK analysis of the State Pension triple lock against alternative uprating rules (double lock, earnings link, CPI link), 2027-28 to 2034-35.

- **Costing**: the fiscal cost of each rule on the OBR central forecast, gross and net of Pension Credit, Housing Benefit and tax interactions.
- **Distribution**: impact by income quintile and decile, region, household type, tenure and age band.
- **Uncertainty**: a Monte Carlo over the OBR's historical forecast errors for CPI and earnings, showing the distribution of the triple lock's cost rather than a single number.

The triple lock applies to the basic and new State Pension only. Additional State Pension is CPI-uprated, and the Pension Credit guarantee follows earnings.
