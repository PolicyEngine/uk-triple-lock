The Burnham plan and the State Pension triple lock

We cost the Prime Minister's plan to adjust the State Pension triple lock from April 2030, as DWP describes it, with full PolicyEngine UK runs. On the OBR's central forecast it saves £0.66bn in 2039-40, because earnings lead in almost every year. Averaged over 50,000 paths of prices and earnings calibrated to the OBR's forecast, 198 of them run in full, the expected saving that year is £8.41bn in State Pension spending and £5.39bn net of tax and benefits; DWP puts it at £15bn on one path.
